# Domain adapters

Select one primary domain and record any secondary adapter used.

## Health

Prioritize guidelines, systematic reviews, randomized trials, high-quality cohorts, and validated databases as appropriate to the question. Track population, baseline status, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcomes, follow-up, absolute effects, harms, and certainty. Do not convert associations into treatment claims. Flag contraindications, interactions, missing diagnostics, and when clinician review is needed.

## Science and technology

Prefer papers, official documentation, standards, benchmarks with reproducible methods, and source code. Separate demonstrated capability from extrapolation. Check benchmark contamination, task validity, baselines, ablations, compute/data differences, and replication.

## Company and market

Prefer filings, regulator records, audited reports, first-party product/pricing documentation, and clearly sourced datasets. Separate company claims from verified performance. State date and currency, distinguish bookings/revenue/cash flow, and expose denominator, survivorship, and selection bias.

## Macro and policy

Prefer statistical agencies, central banks, legislation, court/regulator material, and transparent datasets. Record vintage, revisions, nominal versus real values, seasonal adjustment, base effects, and jurisdiction. Separate forecast scenarios from observed data.

## Marketing

Separate stable principles from platform-specific tactics. Date every tactic claim and downgrade stale platform evidence. Prefer incrementality tests and unit economics over engagement anecdotes. Surface selection bias, attribution leakage, incentives, and closed arbitrage.

## Creative practice

Separate factual history and tool capability from aesthetic judgment. Use primary works, artist statements, documentation, and reproducible examples. State licensing and provenance constraints. Preserve space for subjective evaluation instead of manufacturing consensus.
