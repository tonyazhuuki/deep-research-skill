# Pipeline

## Run contract

Before searching, write:

- question and decision supported;
- mode and domain;
- 3–5 research streams;
- inclusion/exclusion criteria and freshness window;
- personal-data and external-tool boundary;
- deliverables and stopping conditions;
- time, source, and output-token budget when relevant.

## Cycle 1 — landscape

Map distinct evidence streams. For each stream capture candidate claims, strongest sources, quantitative results, limitations, counterevidence, and gaps. Prefer primary evidence and authoritative datasets over commentary.

Required outputs:

- stream notes or an equivalent evidence table;
- `_critic_review.md` attacking scope, hidden assumptions, and missing alternatives;
- `_methods_review.md` assessing design quality and applicability;
- Reflection 1 in `_PROGRESS_LOG.md` selecting the next investigations by expected information value.

## Cycle 2 — targeted depth

Choose 2–3 questions that can materially change the conclusion. Prioritize contested claims, null results, causal mechanisms, boundary conditions, subgroup applicability, and missing personal facts in personalized mode.

Reflection 2 must state:

- what changed since Cycle 1;
- which claims converged, weakened, or remain disputed;
- what evidence would still change the answer;
- whether another search has positive expected value.

## Cycle 3 — synthesis

Integrate across claims; do not concatenate stream summaries. Separate:

- established findings;
- plausible but uncertain interpretations;
- unresolved conflicts;
- decisions or experiments that follow;
- unknowns that block stronger conclusions.

## Stop when

Stop after all required artifacts and gates pass and one of these is true:

- new independent sources no longer change material claims;
- remaining uncertainty is irreducible with available evidence;
- the declared budget is reached and limitations are explicit.

Do not stop merely because many sources were collected. Do not continue merely to fill a cycle.

## Reviews

`_fact_check.md` samples every high-impact claim and every precise number. `_citation_audit.md` checks claim support, source identity, retractions/corrections where relevant, and citation completeness. `_domain_review.md` applies the domain adapter. `_action_map.md` distinguishes evidence-backed options from owner decisions and never mutates operational systems.
